# Privacy Policy — Islami

_Last updated: November 12, 2026_

Islami is a free app for reading the Holy Quran, prayer times and adhan, azkar,
hadith, a tasbih counter and the qibla direction. This policy explains what
the app does with your information.

## Summary

- The app has **no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking**.
- We **do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share** any personal data.
- Everything you do in the app (settings, tasbih counts, reading progress,
  bookmarks, favorite hadiths) is saved **only on your device**.

## Location

If you allow it, the app reads your device location to calculate prayer times
and the qibla direction. The calculation happens **on your device**. The
coordinates are saved on your device so prayer times stay correct when you
open the app again, and they are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can
refuse location access and choose a city manually in Settings instead.

## Notifications

Adhan, prayer reminders and azkar reminders are scheduled locally on your
device. No push notification service is used.

## Internet access

Some content is downloaded from public services when you use it:

| Content | Service |
| --- | --- |
| Full recitations and live Quran radio | mp3quran.net, qurango.net |
| Audio of single ayahs | everyayah.com |
| Hadith texts | cdn.jsdelivr.net (hadith-api) |

Like any website, these services receive your IP address and the file you
request so they can send it. The app does not send them any other information
about you. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.

## Deleting your data

All data is stored on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling
the app removes it completely.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address
with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's issue page:
https://github.com/Marwan-Saif/Islami/issues

# سياسة الخصوصية — إسلامي

_آخر تحديث: ١٢ نوفمبر ٢٠٢٦_

- التطبيق **من غير حسابات ولا إعلانات ولا أي أدوات تتبع أو تحليلات**.
- إحنا **مش بنجمع ولا بنخزن على سيرفرات ولا بنبيع ولا بنشارك** أي بيانات شخصية.

  الأحاديث المفضلة) بتتحفظ **على جهازك بس**.

القبلة **على جهازك**، والإحداثيات بتتحفظ على الجهاز ومش بتتبعت لينا ولا لأي حد.
تقدر ترفض وتختار مدينتك يدوي من الإعدادات.

**الإشعارات:** الأذان والتذكيرات بتتجدول على الجهاز نفسه.

**الإنترنت:** التلاوات والراديو من mp3quran.net و qurango.net، وصوت الآيات من everyayah.com،
ونصوص الأحاديث من cdn.jsdelivr.net. الخدمات دي بتشوف عنوان الـ IP بتاعك والملف
المطلوب بس، زي أي موقع.

**مسح البيانات:** مسح بيانات التطبيق أو إلغاء تثبيته بيمسح كل حاجة.
