## English (en-US)

**App name (max 30):**
Athar: Quran, Adhan & Azkar

**Short description (max 80):**
Quran with tafsir, prayer times & adhan, azkar and hadith. No ads.

**Full description:**

Athar (أثر) is a calm daily companion for worship: the Quran with tafsir, prayer times and the adhan, azkar, hadith, a tasbih counter and the qibla, all in one app with no ads.

📖 The Holy Quran
• The full mushaf in Uthmani script, laid out by page with page and juz numbers
• Tafsir Al-Muyassar for every ayah, and an optional English translation (Saheeh International)
• Listen to any ayah in 20 voices, or play from an ayah to the end of the surah
• Search surah names and the text of the ayahs
• Khatma progress, a daily wird goal, "continue reading" and bookmarks

🕌 Prayer times and adhan
• Accurate times calculated on your phone for your location or a city you pick
• Several calculation methods and the Asr madhab
• The adhan at every prayer: full, short, a soft tone or silent, with a switch per prayer
• A reminder 5 to 30 minutes before each prayer
• The Hijri date

🤲 Azkar
• Morning, evening, prayer, sleep, waking up, mosque and food azkar
• How many times each zekr is said, with a tap counter that counts with you
• Daily reminders for the morning and evening azkar

📚 Hadith
• Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, the four Sunan, Muwatta Malik, An-Nawawi's 40 and the Qudsi hadiths
• Organized by chapter, available offline after the first open, with favorites

📻 Radio and recitations
• Live Quran radio stations
• Full recitations by hundreds of reciters and narrations, with background playback

📿 Tasbih and 🧭 Qibla
• A tasbih counter that keeps your count
• A qibla compass for your location

✨ And more
• Light and dark mode (or follow the phone)
• Arabic and English
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone

## Play Console answers

**Data safety**
- Does the app collect or share user data? **No.** Location is used only on the device
  to calculate prayer times and the qibla, and it is never sent anywhere. Under Google's
  definition, data processed only on the device is not "collected".
- Is data encrypted in transit? Yes. All network requests use HTTPS.
- Can users ask for their data to be deleted? Nothing is stored off the device.
  Uninstalling or clearing the app's storage removes everything.

**App access:** all features work without a login.
**Ads:** No ads.
**Content rating:** answer "No" to every question (religious reference content, no user interaction, no violence). Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.
**Target audience:** 13+ (or 18+). Don't choose an under-13 audience, or the app falls under the Families policy.
**News app:** No. **Health app:** No. **Government app:** No.

**Permissions declarations**
- *Foreground service: media playback* (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`): used to keep
  Quran radio and recitations playing in the background with notification controls.
  Play asks for a short video: start a recitation, press Home, and show the notification controls.
- *Exact alarms* (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`): no declaration is needed (`USE_EXACT_ALARM` was removed).
  The user grants it from inside the app so the adhan plays on time.
- Location: foreground only, no background location.
